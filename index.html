Chapter one

The Girl Who Made Me Nervous

I still remember the first time I saw you — you were scolding someone(we both know) in tuition class and honestly? I was a little scared of you. I never imagined that this girl, the one who could quiet an entire room, would one day become the person I couldn't imagine a day without.

"You started coming in my note list, which was always same. That's when I knew — this couldn't be a coincidence."

You massaged me firs, then I messaged you saying "what a coincidence, our music taste matches" — not knowing it was you behind that account. Not knowing that this small message was going to change everything. We talked for weeks before I even knew who you were. And when I found out it was the girl from tuition class — the same one — I couldn't believe how the universe works sometimes.

Chapter two

Talking Until Mid Night

We became friends. Then close friends. Then the kind of close where you forget time exists. Talking every single day, at every class — you'd look at me, and I'd look at you, but I'd always made sure you wouldn't catch me looking. I was that careful with my heart around you.

Even during exam season, we'd talk late into the night. We could forget to eat, forget everything — but never forget to talk to each other. That meant something. That meant everything.

"We may have forgot to eat, but there was no chance we'd forget to talk."

Then came the day you asked me to meet — and I was so, so shy. I said no at first. You kept asking. And when I finally said yes, you fed me with your own hands. I still feel that warmth. That was our first date, and I didn't have words for how full my heart felt.

You were the one who proposed first. Because of course you were — brave, bold, completely yourself. I loved that about you from the start. Our second meeting gave me something I'll carry my whole life: our first kiss. And yes, you had to go first — because I was still too shy to move. Thank you for that.

Chapter three

Farewells, Board Exams, and the Distance Between Us

We had our college farewell — and we made a date plan after it. It went exactly as we hoped. That felt like a perfect chapter.

Then came the 12th boards, and after that — your dream. CA. Kathmandu. I was genuinely happy for you. I am still happy for you. Watching someone you love chase something this big and this hard is beautiful. I never wanted to be the reason you slowed down.

"I was happy for her. She was chasing her dream. I was supporting that — with my whole heart."

But distance is hard. Not because we loved each other less — I don't believe that for a second. It's hard because when you're used to talking every moment of every day, silence feels louder than it should. When I stopped getting replies and kept seeing you active elsewhere, it didn't make me angry at you — it made me miss you. That's different. I was never fighting you. I was fighting the distance.

I know CA is one of the hardest things anyone can take on. I never questioned your dedication. I only wished, quietly, for a little corner of your day.

✦
Chapter four

The Part That Broke Me — and What Came After

That Saturday — when I came home from church and found(not found but still it was like a surprise so) myself blocked everywhere — I won't pretend that didn't destroy me. I cried like I never had before. Not out of anger. Out of not understanding. Out of loving you and suddenly having no way to reach you at all.

I made another account just to see if you were okay(yours posts followers and following list). I messaged "hello, are you fine?" — setting aside everything I felt — because you mattered more than my pride. I kept my ego aside even when your reply was just "?". I kept going until it was clear you didn't want to talk, and then I stopped.

"Even when she left, I checked her profile every day — not out of obsession, but because letting go of someone you love takes longer than a single decision."

Then came my birthday. You messaged me — the fourth to wish me, at 12:06. I had been waiting. I didn't say that. I just talked to you nicely, set aside everything, and asked why you left. You told me someone had convinced you I wasn't right for you.

I forgave you. Not because it didn't hurt. It did. But because I love you and I couldn't imagine choosing anything else. When you came back home for a while and we met — I hid everything I felt inside and acted normal. But deep down, I was so happy just to see your face again.

Chapter five

Not Complaints — Honest Feelings

I want to say something carefully, because I don't want this to sound like a list of things you did wrong. It isn't. You haven't done everything wrong. And neither have I been perfect — I know I've hurt you too, in ways I carry with me. I'm sorry for those.

But I am human. I need attention sometimes. I need to feel like I exist in your world even when we're far apart. When I reply to you instantly and your reply comes days later, I don't resent you — I just feel invisible. When I called and you minimized the screen, I didn't get angry — I just stopped calling, because I didn't want to feel like a burden.

"I'm not asking for all of your time. Just enough to know I still matter."

The trip moment — you went somewhere without telling me, and I went somewhere without telling you. We both did the same thing. Maybe that says something: we've both been trying to protect ourselves. I get it. I really do.

And when I share something about myself and get a laughing reaction and no reply — it stings. Not because I want validation, but because I want to feel close to you. That's all. That has always been all.

I've been learning to give you your space. To not check my phone every five minutes. To accept that your life is full and hard and demanding. I'm proud of you for that life you're building. I just hope there's still a little place in it for me.

✦
💛

"Even After Everything — I Choose You."

This isn't a complaint letter. This is a love letter. These are the thoughts of someone who has loved you from the moment you started copying note songs — and hasn't stopped, not once, not even on the hardest days.

I know I'm not perfect. I know I've made mistakes. I know our story isn't always easy. But I also know that when I think about the future, you're in it. That hasn't changed.

Thank you for being brave enough to message first, to propose first, to kiss first. Thank you for our late night talks, for feeding me with your hands, for wishing me on my birthday even after everything. Thank you for being exactly who you are — complicated, ambitious, real.

I am choosing you. I will always choose you. That's the simplest thing I know.

Yours, always and completely 💛
